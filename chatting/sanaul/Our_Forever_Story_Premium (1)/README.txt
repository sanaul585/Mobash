OUR FOREVER STORY ❤️
Premium digital gift website.

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/

Features:
- Cinematic romantic landing page
- Light/dark mode
- Falling hearts
- Animated envelope and love letter
- Story chapters
- Engagement/rism section
- Interactive gallery photo upload
- Future dreams
- Final forever surprise
- Mobile responsive

Open index.html in Chrome.

To personalize:
1. Edit names/messages/date in index.html.
2. Use the Gallery boxes to preview your own photos.
3. For permanent hosted photos, place images in assets/ and replace the gallery backgrounds with image paths.
